# Standalone Web editorial art direction

The approved About page is the reference. Home and immersive Corridor are separate surfaces and are not affected by this system.

## Locked: grounded dimensional artwork

- Every sculptural object or character rests on a plausible horizontal floor, not a wall or empty space.
- Build attached contact occlusion at every weight-bearing edge into the raster alpha, followed by a soft, perspective-correct projected shadow. Preserve that alpha during optimisation.
- Use About's blue/chrome UKD sculpture as the lighting, material and grounding reference. No rectangular background fill, halo or generic detached ellipse.
- Keep object and shadow together, stationary. `GroundedSculpture` supplies only a restrained moving surface light, paused offscreen, in background tabs and for reduced-motion users.
- Inspect the contact points at desktop and mobile sizes in both themes. No clipping or floating gap.

Use the shared editorial hero, spacing and typography for equivalent standalone marketing pages. Preserve real project imagery, secure forms and page-specific controls. Do not introduce numbered decoration, ambient grid overlays or repeated generic card stacks.
