Critical Illness & Accident

Extra protection for the expenses health plans do not always solve.

Health insurance pays for medical care. Supplemental accident and critical illness cover can pay you directly, helping with the costs that arrive around a hard moment.

The difference

Medical cover pays for care. This pays for life around it.

Two kinds of protection, designed for two different kinds of cost.

Health insurance

Medical coverage

Pays doctors, hospitals and pharmacies for covered care, after your share.

Supplemental

Financial support

Pays a set cash amount to you after a covered event, to use as you choose.

Two scenarios

Where each kind of cover helps.

Accident cover

A broken wrist on a weekend hike

Medical coverage
Your health plan helps with the emergency visit, imaging and follow-up care.
Supplemental support
An accident policy may pay a set cash benefit you can use however you need.

Beyond the bill

The expenses that follow a hard moment.

Many costs around an accident or illness are not medical at all. They are the reason supplemental cover exists.

  1. Travel

    Trips to a specialist centre, parking and accommodation for family.

  2. Recovery time

    Unpaid leave, reduced hours or time away from self-employed work.

  3. Household disruption

    Childcare, help at home and the everyday bills that keep arriving.

Decision guide

When it may be worth considering.

Supplemental cover is personal. It can be valuable in one household and unnecessary in the next. These signals suggest it deserves a closer look.

Educational, not advice

Benefits, definitions and exclusions vary by policy. Review the full terms, or speak with a licensed professional, before deciding.

  • You have a high deductible. A cash benefit can help cover your share of a large bill.
  • Your savings are limited. Supplemental cover can ease the financial shock of a sudden event.
  • Your family history concerns you. Critical illness cover is designed around specific diagnoses.
  • Your work is physical or active. Accident cover may be more relevant to your everyday risk.
