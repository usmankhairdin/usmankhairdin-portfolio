How health cover works

Three decisions

Better coverage choices come in three parts.

Every plan decision is really three smaller ones. Sanaira keeps them in order, so each one is easier to make.

  1. Understand your care

    Map the visits, prescriptions and people your coverage needs to support before comparing anything.

    • Doctors and clinics you trust
    • Prescriptions and refills
    • Planned and possible care

  2. Compare the true cost

    Look past the monthly premium to what a typical year — and a harder one — could actually cost.

    • Premium plus expected care
    • Deductible exposure
    • Out-of-pocket ceilings

  3. Add only what matters

    Build around a protected core, then add dental, vision or supplemental cover only where it earns its place.

    • A strong medical foundation
    • Optional, explained add-ons
    • No bundled extras by default
