Savings Estimator

A clearer starting point for your coverage budget.

Before you compare plans, get a sense of the range that might fit your household. This demo estimator shows how an estimate is built — it is not a quote or an eligibility decision.

Build my estimateHow we compare plans

What shapes an estimate

Four inputs, one honest range.

An estimate is only as useful as its inputs. These are the factors most coverage estimates start from.

  1. Household size

    Who you are covering changes both the plans available and their cost.

  2. Where you live

    Plan options and prices vary by location, often down to the ZIP code.

  3. Age ranges

    Premiums are generally priced by age band.

  4. Income range

    In some markets, income can affect the help available toward premiums. Real eligibility must be confirmed with the official source for your area.

Not an eligibility check

This template does not calculate subsidies, tax credits or eligibility for any programme. Real figures must come from the official source for your location.

Privacy first

Built to ask for less.

Sensitive questions deserve restraint. The estimator asks for ranges rather than exact figures, and the template ships with no analytics or tracking.

  • Nothing is saved. The demo form keeps no record of what you enter.
  • Ranges, not records. Income and age are chosen as ranges, never exact figures.
  • You stay in control. In a live product, you would decide what is stored and who can see it.
This demo form stores nothing.

Every field resets when you leave the page. No figure you choose is sent anywhere.

  • No account required
