Major medical is the foundation.

It protects against the costs that are hardest to recover from: hospital stays, surgery, ongoing treatment and serious illness. Everything else is built on top of it, never instead of it.

The coverage map

Layers that add support where it’s needed.

Each optional layer answers a specific need that medical insurance does not. If the need isn’t part of your life, the layer isn’t part of your plan.

Life changes

Different moments, different layers.

Coverage needs move with life. Choose a moment to see what usually deserves a closer look.

When your household grows

Paediatric visits, vaccinations and dental care for children become regular parts of the year. Look for networks that include family doctors and consider dental cover early.

  • Dental & Vision
  • Compare Plans

When you work for yourself

Without an employer plan, you choose and fund your own cover. A predictable core plan plus a clear budget matters more than ever.

  • Savings Estimator
  • Health Insurance

When you move or travel

Networks are local. A move can change which doctors are in network and which plans are available — review cover before the move, not after.

  • Short-Term Cover
  • Compare Plans

When your health changes

Specialists, prescriptions and treatment plans make deductible and out-of-pocket limits more important than the monthly premium.

  • Compare Plans
  • Critical Illness & Accident

When work winds down

Leaving employer cover before other benefits begin can create a gap. Plan the bridge and the long-term cover together.
