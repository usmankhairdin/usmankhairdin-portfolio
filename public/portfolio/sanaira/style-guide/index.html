Care Weave

One object, two behaviours: the homepage journey (scroll-driven) and the still composition (idle self-turn only).

StillIdle only.Turns slowly, pauses off-screen

Motion & reduced motion

  1. Homepage

    Scroll-driven Care Weave journey, animated hero mesh. Reduced motion shows composed still chapters.

  2. Content pages

    One-time copy reveals, hover arrows, idle weave stills at a slow 44–64 s turn. Paused off-screen.

  3. Auth & wizard

    Calm mesh field and idle weave; wizard steps slide horizontally inside a stable card.

  4. Reduced motion

    All transitions and idle motion stop; content is fully visible without animation.

Motion durations and easing live in tokens.css (--ease-care) and component stylesheets.
