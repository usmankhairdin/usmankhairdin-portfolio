Providers & medications

What matters to clients, checked per plan.

Add a client’s doctors and medications once. Every plan in the comparison shows network status and prescription costs beside it, and flags anything out of network — ready to discuss, not to research.

  • Doctor and clinic network checks
  • Prescription costs by plan
  • Flags for anything out of network

Renewals

Every renewal, seen in good time.

The renewal pipeline lists each household renewing in the next 90 days with its current plan, what changes and your next follow-up — so review conversations start early, never in a rush.

  • Renewals due in 30, 60 and 90 days
  • Premium and network changes to review
  • The next follow-up for each household

Data & relationships

Your clients, your relationships.

Sanaira is designed so that trust flows in the right direction — from clients, to their broker.

  1. Clients decide what they share

    Households choose what their broker can see, and can change it.

  2. Visibility follows assignment

    Advisors see their own clients by default; sharing is deliberate.

  3. Relationships stay with you

    Your client list is yours. The platform never markets to it.
