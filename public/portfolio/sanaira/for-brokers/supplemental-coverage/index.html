Supplemental categories

Four kinds of cover, four kinds of household.

Each category fits a recognisable need. Knowing which households it suits keeps every recommendation relevant.

  1. Dental

    Preventive and restorative care, often a family priority with children.

  2. Vision

    Exams and eyewear for households who use them every year.

  3. Accident

    Cash support for active households and physical work.

  4. Critical illness

    A lump sum for clients with limited savings or family history.

Single client view

Core and add-ons, side by side.

Every add-on sits in the same quote as the health plan, beside the client’s saved needs, so a household sees how dental and vision or accident cover adds to the monthly total — and you can explain it in one conversation.
