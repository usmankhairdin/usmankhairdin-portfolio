Planning a change?

Talk it through

Budget predictability

With a contribution-based approach you set the allowance per employee class, so the maximum monthly cost is known in advance. Actual reimbursements depend on what employees choose, up to that amount.

Commonly by defined employee classes — for example full-time and part-time. The rules for classes vary, so confirm them with a qualified advisor.

You review participation and budget, then decide whether to keep or change allowances. There is no group renewal increase imposed on the plan itself.

Employee choice

They choose from individual plans available where they live, so many can select a plan whose network includes their current doctors.

Guided comparisons and real people are part of the Sanaira concept. Employees can compare plans by doctors, prescriptions and total yearly cost.

Broker involvement

Yes. The broker workspace is designed for advisors to guide employers and employees through setup, selection and renewal.

Depending on your setup, your broker, Sanaira guides or both. Responsibilities are agreed before rollout.

Rollout and support

The illustrative timeline is about six weeks, from strategy session to coverage starting. Your own timeline depends on plan year and policy decisions.

An announcement, a live Q&A, a recorded walkthrough and a guided setup in the workspace.

Eligibility and policy considerations

Not necessarily. Size, locations, existing plans and local rules all matter. Treat this page as an introduction, not advice.

Reimbursement arrangements are governed by regulations that vary by market. This template includes placeholder content only — obtain licensed, market-specific advice before any real deployment.
