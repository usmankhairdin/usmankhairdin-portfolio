ICHRA Benefits

A more flexible way to contribute to benefits.

An individual coverage health reimbursement arrangement lets employers contribute a set amount toward the individual coverage each employee chooses. Here is the idea, explained simply.

In plain language

What an ICHRA is, in one paragraph.

Illustrative, educational content

Eligibility, employee classes, affordability and reimbursement rules are set by regulation and vary. Confirm every detail with qualified legal and benefits advisors for your market before acting.

Four steps

Simple to explain, simple to run.

The arrangement is easier to understand as a sequence than as a definition.
