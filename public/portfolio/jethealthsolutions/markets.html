Serve all Customers via a Single Platform

JET Insure brings you a comprehensive plan management system that helps your team to present and sell all types of health plans and ancillary insurance products to individual and group customers.

ACA Marketplace

For your individual and group customers shopping for ACA on-exchange or off-exchange plans, use our JET Insure platform for customers to select the best health plan as per their needs, budget, and requirements. JET Insure allows you to present and sell ACA (Affordable Care Act) insurance products to your prospects. With a complete control over presentation and branding, automate quote to card policy administration, and improve sales efficiency. Full support for Enhance Direct Enrollment available to enrich user experience.

Fully-Funded / Fully Insured

With JET Insure, you can serve individual as well as group customers. Allow your customers to pick the right type of healthcare plan – Fully Funded or other alternative available options such as Self-Funded plans within your product portfolio for their organization and employees. Rich customizable tool, group and member portals enable easy add/remove employee/dependent features, open enrollment, Cobra management. Use JET Insure to make policy management an hassle-free experience.

Self-Funded / Stop Loss

For the employers interested in Self-Funded options, JET Insure gives you out-of-the-box capability to offer Self-funded plans to your customer alongside other alternative options such as the Fully-Funded plans. We bring you the best configurable marketplace supporting variety of healthcare products (whether managed by the insurance carrier or TPA) with extended features to manage cost and stop loss.
