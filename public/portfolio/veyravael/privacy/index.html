Privacy information

Privacy, plainly explained.

Clear information about using the Veyravael website.

A clear starting point

You can explore the collection without sharing personal information. This website uses no analytics, advertising scripts or third-party trackers.

Information entered in forms

Information entered in a form is not retained on this device. Passwords are never stored in browser storage.

Your local shortlist

If browser storage is available, only the identifiers of saved residences and your cookie preference are stored on this device. Remove saved residences using the heart button. You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Images and outside links

Fonts, photographs and runtime libraries are served locally. The creator credit opens an external website, which has its own policies.

Your choices

You can remove individual saved residences with the heart button, manage preferences below, or clear this website’s data in your browser settings.
