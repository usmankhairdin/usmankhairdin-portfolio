Stuff You Should Know

Whether you’re getting work done or doing tasks on FixerPal, know that
we’ve got your back every step of the way.



Secure Payments

We hold task payments secure with our PCI-DSS compliant FixerPal Pay – so tasks can be completed knowing payment is there when you're done.

Top rated insurance

Insurance is there to ease any worries - making sure the Fixer has liability insurance from CGU while performing most task activities. T&C's apply.

Verified badges

Badges give members a bit more verified info when deciding who to work with on a task. Each badge has certain requirements that must be met and verified before they’re shown on the member's profile.

Here if you need us

Our comprehensive Help Centre and dedicated FixerPal Support are on hand 24/7 to help with any questions, queries or issues you might have.
