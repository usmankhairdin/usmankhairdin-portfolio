BEFORE WE BEGIN05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity.
A good beginning.

A few things you might be wondering.
For everything else, let’s talk.

Start a conversation

Start with a few details about your space, what you would like to change and what matters most to you. The next step would be an initial conversation to understand the brief, discuss feasibility and agree how to explore it further.

The studio concept covers kitchens, bathrooms, living spaces, existing loft renovations, whole-home projects and bespoke joinery. The right scope depends on the property, your priorities and the specialists a project needs.

Design intent, technical details and the build programme should be considered together. A clear brief, coordinated drawings and regular conversations between design, project management and specialist trades help keep decisions connected.

A realistic programme follows the scope, surveys, design decisions, permissions where relevant and material lead times. Key milestones and any periods of disruption should be discussed before building begins, then reviewed as the work progresses.

Begin with a comfortable overall range and your most important outcomes. Allow for design, construction, fittings, any professional fees and a sensible contingency. A detailed scope helps distinguish essentials from options before you commit.
