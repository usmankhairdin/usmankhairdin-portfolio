His Majesty King Hamad Bin Isa Al Khalifa

Ignorance is the Enemy of Peace, it is, therefore, our duty to learn, to share, and to live together, by the Tenets of Faith in the spirit of mutual respect and love.

  • Date: Tuesday, October 10, 2017
  • Location: Kingdom of Bahrain

H.E. Dr. Shaikh Khalid bin Khalifa Al Khalifa

Chairman of the Board of Trustees

Throughout its 5000 years of illustrious history, The Kingdom of Bahrain has been a welcoming oasis for all who arrived on her shores.

Bahrain’s strategic location in the Arabian Gulf, bridging the cultures of East and West, has resulted in a multi-cultural society comprised of many faiths and ethnicities. Peaceful coexistence is the normal and natural way of life in Bahrain today, just as it has been for Millenia and this rich mosaic of cultural diversity has seen Bahrain develop into a modern and progressive Kingdom, thriving, prosperous and peaceful under the wise leadership of His Majesty King Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa.
