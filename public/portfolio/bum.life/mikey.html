Mikey – The Prankster

The wild card of the crew. Life is one big prank to him and he thrives on turning dull moments into chaos.

Key Characteristics

Strength: Can make anyone laugh, lightens tense situations
Flaw: Does not know when to stop, pranks often backfire
Quirk: Carries random junk for pranks such as rubber chicken or fake cash
Vibe: Chaotic, playful, troublemaker

Extended Personality

Mikey is the guy who treats life like a never ending TikTok prank channel. He will tape a kick me sign on Richie, swap Phil's cup of soup with dirty puddle water, and laugh until he is in tears. Half the time the jokes flop and the crew pays for it, but Mikey lives for the chaos. Deep down, he just wants people to laugh with him and not at him.
