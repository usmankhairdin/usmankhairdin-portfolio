Jamal – The Street Hustler

Street smart hustler with a silver tongue. Always scheming to turn scraps into something valuable.

Key Characteristics

Strength: Resourceful, quick talker, knows how to survive
Flaw: Can be selfish, puts himself first
Quirk: Constantly counting cash he does not have
Vibe: Realist, sarcastic, hustler

Extended Personality

Jamal has street hustler energy. He always has a side scheme running, whether it is selling so-called gourmet hotdogs he found or trying to rent out an alley corner to tourists. He is smooth with words, quick with sarcasm, and knows how to stretch a dollar. But when things get tense, he is the first to call out the nonsense and remind the group that life on the street is not a comedy skit.
