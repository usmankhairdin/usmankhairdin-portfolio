Phil – The Philosopher

The deep thinker who turns trash into metaphors. He is crazy wise or just crazy.

Key Characteristics

Strength: Drops unexpected wisdom, sees meaning in everything
Flaw: Rambles endlessly, often ignored
Quirk: Talks to pigeons as if they are disciples
Vibe: Absurd, wise, eccentric

Extended Personality

Phil is the guy who can look at a half eaten slice of pizza and somehow turn it into a metaphor for human existence. He is chill, half crazy, but sometimes he says things that actually make sense, usually when nobody is listening. The crew often rolls their eyes when he rambles, but every now and then his trash bag wisdom saves their asses.
