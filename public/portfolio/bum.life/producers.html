Project Concept

Bum.Life is a groundbreaking animated series that brings unprecedented authenticity to the genre. Set against the backdrop of urban street life, five homeless friends navigate the chaos of survival with razor-sharp wit, unbreakable bonds, and beer-fueled schemes that hilariously backfire.

What sets Bum.Life apart is our commitment to raw storytelling. Drawing from real experiences and authentic voices from the streets, we don't romanticize homelessness—we humanize it. Every episode blends dark comedy with heartfelt moments, creating a series that's as thought-provoking as it is entertaining.

This isn't just another animated show—it's a cultural phenomenon waiting to happen. With its unique blend of social commentary and laugh-out-loud humor, Bum.Life is positioned to capture both critical acclaim and massive audience engagement.
