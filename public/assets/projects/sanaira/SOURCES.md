# Sanaira Health portfolio evidence

Captured from https://sanaira.usmankhairdin.com/ on 13 October 2026.
Desktop captures are native 1440 × 900; phone captures are 390 × 844.
The smaller WebP variants are downsampled originals. No interface content is redrawn.

Website: `/`, `/for-individuals/`, `/for-individuals/compare-plans/`,
`/for-brokers/platform/`, `/for-teams/ichra-benefits/`, `/find-coverage/`, `/plans/`.

Application: `/app/broker/`, `/app/broker/clients/`, `/app/broker/clients/ellis/`,
`/app/broker/quotes/`, `/app/broker/quotes/q-ellis/`, `/app/broker/enrollments/`,
`/app/broker/enrollments/e-ellis/`, `/app/broker/renewals/`, `/app/broker/documents/`,
`/app/broker/messages/t-ellis/`, `/app/member/`, `/app/employer/`.

Both themes are captured for the homepage and broker overview.
The full-page homepage capture is the static browser rendering of a website whose
Care Weave normally travels in a fixed WebGL scene as the visitor scrolls.

The animated brand section reuses the project's original `weave-still-scene.js`
and `continuum-math.js`, with only the canvas class and theme notification adapted
to the portfolio. The accompanying fallback images are original Sanaira assets.

Sanaira is a fictional brand. All people, plans, prices and figures in its website
and application are demo content, not live insurance services or client outcomes.
